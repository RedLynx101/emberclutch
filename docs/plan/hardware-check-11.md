# Run 26: the banner, its joints closed (0.10.2)

**0.10.2, Emberclutch: Skyreach Valley.** Your run 25 notes:
- **The banner freezes were its size.** The two over 500 KB froze (25B, 25C); every one at 494 KB or less held,
  even the Blazeplume. From now on banners stay under 480 KB.
- **Your pick, 25F (the Pouncer), with its joints closed:** each moving piece is closed with skin where it
  was cut, overlaps the body further, and moves a little more gently (the head tilts 8 degrees, the tail wags
  16), so nothing should split open. 26A is that; 26B is the Blazeplume (25E) done the same way.
- **Tam's rod** is drawn in 3D now, so you and your dragons stand in front of it properly.
- **Hats** can go down to half size: the Crestwing's and Curlstone's now sit small on their heads among their
  crests instead of floating.
