# Code of Conduct

*Emberclutch* is a cozy game about caring for something, and its community should feel the same way.

## Our pledge
We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy community.

## Our standards
Examples of behaviour that helps:
- being kind and patient with others, especially people new to homebrew or to programming;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the game.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit
  permission;
- sharing or asking for pirated games, keys or copyrighted firmware in the project's spaces;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and enforcement
The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and
fair action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. The
maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't
align with this Code of Conduct, and will say why when appropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies in all of the project's spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests
and discussions) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting
Report behaviour that breaks this code to the maintainer, Noah Hicks, by email at
**NoahHicks101@gmail.com** (or [@RedLynx101](https://github.com/RedLynx101) on GitHub). All reports are
reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Consequences
1. **Correction:** a private, written warning that explains the problem, and possibly a public apology.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, such as a time without interaction
   with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards or disparagement of
   groups of people.
