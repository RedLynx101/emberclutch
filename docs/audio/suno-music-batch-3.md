# Emberclutch — Suno Music Brief, Batch 3 (Beta: *The valley*)

**For Noah, 2026-09-25** (Beta WP15, sent early so the music arrives ahead of the valley
work). Same setup as [batch 1](suno-music-brief.md): **Suno v5.5, paid plan, Custom Mode,
Instrumental ON**, Weirdness ~30%, Style Influence ~75%, the same Exclude Styles list
(`vocals, singing, lyrics, spoken word, rap, electric guitar, dubstep, trap drums, EDM drop`).
ElevenLabs Music is fine too wherever it gives the cleaner take (as for `hatching`).
Download **WAV** and drop the files in `assets/audio/music/source/` with the slugs below.

What's already in hand and stays: `title-theme`, `den-hearth` (the den by day), `nestsong`
(night, eggs), `skyreach` + `skyreach-2` (flying and riding), `cup-day` (the arena),
`market-bustle` (the Market's shop screen), and the stingers `hatching` and
`wanderings-return`.

Reminders from batches 1–2:
- **Loops:** 2–3 minutes, a steady groove, no big dramatic turns; I cut the loop from the
  middle, and Suno's tempo drift doesn't matter (the seam is found by ear-like matching).
- **Stingers:** short, with a **clean, ringing ending**; that matters more than tempo.
- 2–4 takes per prompt; keep the steadiest.

## Processing (what I do)
- Loops: `make_loop.py` finds the seam, levels to -16 LUFS, encodes Ogg, and writes the
  seam preview to `assets/audio/music/previews/` for you to listen to.
- Stingers: `make_loop.py <wav> --no-loop` trims the lead-in, keeps the natural ending,
  levels and encodes.
- The licence note goes in `assets/audio/music/LICENSE-MUSIC.md` (which tool, which plan).
