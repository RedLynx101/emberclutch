# Run 23: run 22's fixes (0.9.15)

**0.9.15, Emberclutch: Skyreach Valley.** Your run 22 notes, fixed:
- **The Trailhead freeze, found.** The painted trail on its top screen worked out each point's direction
  from a point a little further on, and at the far end that went past the trail's end into a square
  root of a negative number. So the trail's last triangles had impossible corners on every frame. The
  emulator skips them; your 3DS's graphics chip can lock up on them, which is exactly what the watchdog
  recorded (the screen sent, never finished, the music still going), here and in 0.9.11 to 0.9.13. The
  trail now stops at its end.
- **The 3D banner:** *Emberclutch* back where it was before 0.9.13, *Skyreach Valley* under it, the
  hatchling and its egg a little smaller below.
- **The mailbox:** a little music-box chime when you walk up to it with a letter waiting (and the mail
  jingle when one arrives).
- **The people you failed:** Rowan's forehead, the gaps between hat and head (Bram, Fig, Tam), Fig's hat
  and hair, Tam's brow-to-beard band (his hair now shows only at the sides and back), Tove's hair through
  her hat, and Solenne made more like Emilia: an elf, fair skin, violet eyes, silver hair with a lavender
  tint, a white flower, white and lavender with purple.
