# Run 25: the new banner test, and run 24's fixes (0.10.1)

**0.10.1, Emberclutch: Skyreach Valley.** Your run 24 notes, fixed, and six new banners to try:
- **Waking a dragon up.** A sleeping dragon now wakes when you touch it, throw the ball, hold out food or
  rinse it (it says so, with a purr). Nothing counts until it's up, so throwing the ball no longer raises
  Play while it sleeps. At night it stays up for a minute after the last thing you did with it, so you can
  feed, bathe and play; a minute of nothing and it goes back to bed.
- **Your wandering dragon** flaps now: a few wingbeats, a glide, and again. It was near the Trailhead
  because its trip starts and ends there: it circles over wherever your steps have taken it on the loop,
  and it only comes home when you call it back at the Wanderers' Trailhead.
- **The cast's line** no longer snaps straight out on the first frame.
- **Tam** fishes at the water's edge now, his line out in the lake (he stood up the beach by his shack).
- **The Lilyfin's legs** come out of its body now (its shoulders and hips were tucked under the belly).
- **Hats:** on the Lilyfin they sit level on top of its lily pad; a wide brim no longer cuts into the head
  (the Puffback's straw hat rests on its forehead now).
- **The new banner:** a real baby dragon from the game in its egg (full detail, smooth, its real skin, a
  clean ink outline on four of them). Six banner-test titles: 25A Pouncer, 25B Blazeplume, 25C Crestwing,
  25D Puffback (all with the outline), 25E Blazeplume and 25F Pouncer without it.
