How to make them, and where they go

Music:

Exclude Styles:

  • Loops: 2–3 minutes, a steady groove; Suno's tempo drift doesn't matter. Stingers: short, with a clean, ringing end.
  • 2–4 takes per prompt; keep the steadiest. Download WAV, named by the slug, into assets/audio/music/source/.
  • Sounds: ElevenLabs Sound Effects (or Suno Sounds): short and clean, no music, no long reverb. Loops 10–20 s with nothing that stands out. Put them all in one folder and tell Claude; takes can be named anything.
  • Tick a box once a take you like is saved. Anything to tell Claude goes in the notes at the bottom.
