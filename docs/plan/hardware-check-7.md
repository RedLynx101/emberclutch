# Run 22: the Living Valley (0.9.14)

**0.9.14, Emberclutch: Skyreach Valley.** Your planning notes and the storyboard, built:
- **Everyone in Set 2**, the Storybook look: you (both bodies, the six hair styles, two of them new:
  Swept and Braid), the six villagers, and new bodies for Fig, Tam, Tove, Linnet, Madder, Celestine,
  Primrose and the four champions (Marigold, Captain Rook, Seraphine, Solenne in their league colours).
- **Feelings**: whoever speaks shows each line's feeling on their face (ten kinds of eyes: happy arcs,
  squeezed shut, wide, droopy, slanted, sleepy, hearts, sparkles, spirals; their mouth moving with the
  letters; their brows), acts it out (a laugh, a huff, a stomp, a think, a shy sway, hands on hips,
  Celestine's swoon, Rook's cool lean and more), and a little icon pops over their head with a sound.
  The portrait in the box changes with them (calm, happy, sad, angry, surprised).

  Custard and Cinder, Market day, the errands, the festival), and the side lines: the pageant with a
  fiery Celestine and Primrose the rival, the league to champion, the Hollow's count and the Frost
  Warden, Tam and Old Whiskers. The mailbox at the den's door has letters (28 of them, a few only when
  you've done something). Quests notice what you've already done.
- **Growing up**: an egg hatches after a day and a half warm, and the best care grows a dragon in 5.5 days.
- **The league**: beating Solenne makes you one of the five champions of Skyreach Valley (the Hollow
  needn't be finished). Your badges (Ember, Flame, Blaze, Starfire, Champion) are on the profile's
  Record page. Levels top out at 42 here. The champions you've beaten are out in the valley.
- The watchdog stays in (it costs next to nothing). A separate player build (no dev menu, overlay or Y
  screenshots) is kept ready for the repo and releases, not on your 3DS.
- **At the end of this page**, everyone's new look: pass or fail each, with a note.

**Your save:** it carries over. The game moves your progress into the new story when it first loads it
(quests you'd done are done; a letter, "News from the valley", explains). I'll copy your save off the
