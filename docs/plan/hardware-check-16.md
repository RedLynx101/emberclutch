# Run 31: the traits work (1.0.0, the player build)

**1.0.0, Emberclutch: Skyreach Valley**, still the player build. Your run 30: the handbook is good, and it
should tell you what each trait does. They did nothing yet: the game rolled them and showed them, and no part
of it read them. Now every one of the 34 does one thing, on a system already in the game (the proposal and
the full list are below, and in the guide's new almanac):
- **Care and moods:** Tidy stays clean longer; Hearty Eater gets more from food; Cuddly and Chatty build bond
  faster; Water-Lover enjoys its bath; Early Riser, Night Owl and Sunbather hold their Love (and Play) through
  their hours; Deep Sleeper rests faster; Glowheart is joyful more easily; Loyal never minds you being away;
  Phoenix Heart never turns a sulk into being upset.
- **Training:** Quick Learner learns faster; Sturdy tires less; Starborn has a point more in every stat.
- **Flying:** Swift is faster, Skydancer turns tighter and glides further, Strong Wings tires less in the air.
- **The Wanderings:** Keen Nose finds more, Treasure Hunter finds more Gleam, Lucky finds rare things more.
- **Battles:** Ironhide takes less damage, Elemental's own breath hits harder, Warm-Blooded and Cool-Headed
  shrug off Frost and Ember, Brave Heart shrugs off Roars and the like, Moonlit and Sunkissed are stronger at
  night and by day.
- **Shows and challenges:** Showoff and Gentle Giant score more, Songbird keeps time better, Mossback counts
  as Grove for Grove shows, Sure-Footed gets an extra heart in the Lantern Trial.
- **Breeding:** Ancient Blood's eggs come out rare more often.

Every trait is tested to do its thing. One change in balance to know about: Frostspire Hollow's deepest
guardian (floor 30) usually has Ironhide and Cool-Headed, so it's now the Hollow's hardest fight by a clear
step (a dragon a level below it wins about one in eight, where it was one in four).
