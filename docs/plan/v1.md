# Beta and 1.0 together — *The valley* and *A trainer*

**Settled at the sit-down, 2026-09-26 (D89 run 19's notes, D90 the answers).** Beta and 1.0 are
one pass now: finish the valley, fix what run 19 found, and build 1.0's trainer game on it, to a
polished game that works end to end and feels like a game. Worked through in detail with my own
checking (scripted emulator runs, PC tests), then **one large review** of it all, a hardware run
and a small set of banner labs. Tags: `v0.3.0-beta` once the valley half passes, `v1.0.0` at the
end (the public release waits for Noah's word).

## What 1.0 is
A cozy dragon life game on the old 3DS: raise and care for your dragons in the den, take them out
into Skyreach Valley, and train them into champions of **battle** and **beauty**, with the Lantern
Festival as the story that walks you in. Everything a player does feeds one progression: care
builds bond, bond and training raise stats and moves, and they win battles, shows and challenges,
whose rewards buy food, toys, decor and accessories. Each dragon keeps its own record.

## Decided (D90)
- **Battles: turn-based, one dragon each**, right in the valley (the landscape is the arena; you,
  the challenger and your dragons stand there). Four moves a dragon from its elements and its
  training; the five stats drive it: Wing sets who goes first, Might and Breath the power of
  body and breath moves, Stamina the health, Wit accuracy, critical hits and dodges; the elements
  have matchups. Teams of three may come later.
- **The pageant: themed shows.** Three rounds (Look, Poise, Performance) against random
  challengers picked for the league; each show has a theme (a Frost Ball, a Harvest Fair, a
  Starlight Gala ...) favouring certain elements, colours and accessories, so different dragons
  shine in different shows. Look: cleanliness, accessories and how they suit its colours, kind
  and skin rarity. Poise: bond, manner, how well it's cared for. Performance: tricks you cue.
- **Battles and shows each have their own board and leagues** (Ember → Flame → Blaze → Starfire),
  apart from the minigame challenges; every win, ribbon and title is **kept per dragon** and shown
  in its profile.
- **Four new places:** **Emberpeak Caldera** (a volcano's rim in the east: the battle league's
  grand stage and finals), **Moonpetal Glade** (a night garden of glowing flowers: the pageant's
  hall, accessory and dye stalls), **Driftwood Cove** (a sandy cove on the lake's far shore:
  fishing for dragon food, shells, tide finds) and **Frostspire Hollow** (an ice cave in the
  cold heights: **a training ground**, wild dragons to battle for experience, floor by floor
  deeper and harder, its own rewards and a keeper who tracks your progress).
- **The flicker** (run 19): shimmering surfaces, things popping in and out, parts vanishing for a
  moment: a deep pass on depth, culling and streaming.

from the stats (and your figure no longer split in 3D); trophies that mean something. Many more

## The work, in order
1. **Fix and steady** (run 19's list above, the flicker first) — the valley and den as they are,
   solid on the hardware budget (typical valley views ≤ 9,000 triangles).
2. **Care and progression:** Love and Energy; walking together; experience, levels and stat
   growth from training and battles; moves learned; per-dragon records; the save grows (older
   saves carry over).
3. **Battles:** the rules (core, PC-tested), the battle in the valley (camera, moves, effects,
   sounds), challengers about the valley with their own dragons and lines, the Ember → Starfire
   league on its board at the arena, the finals at Emberpeak Caldera.
4. **Training:** Frostspire Hollow (wild dragons, floors, experience, rewards); the training yard
   in the den's valley (moves and tricks taught with the stylus and buttons; voice commands as a
   stretch).
5. **Beauty:** accessories (hats, bows, scarves, collars, wing charms, saddles; bought, won and
   found; worn on the models), dyes; themed shows at Moonpetal Glade in their league.
6. **The valley's life:** textured ground, particles, day and weather touches, Driftwood Cove
   (fishing), random renewing finds, more sounds and music cues.
7. **The interface:** the Market, the Wanderings, the Journal (tracked quests, map markers), the
   tray and profile (records, moves, accessories), a gentle tutorial through the festival's
   first steps, settings.
8. **Economy and balance:** prices, rewards (no farming), what each league unlocks.
9. **Close:** the whole game played through by scripted runs, the checklist, one large review
   page, run 20 with a small set of banner labs (pairs of lab A's parts), the tags.

## Budgets
The old 3DS: typical valley views ≤ 9,000 top-screen triangles; the den ≤ 16.7 ms with three
dragons out; battles and shows draw two dragons and two people close up (≤ 9,000 as well); linear
memory kept clear of kinds nothing uses.
