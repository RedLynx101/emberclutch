# The trailer, V3: the footage and the voices

**The trailer's footage is captured** (docs/plan/trailer.md, V1-V3): every shot of the plan recorded from the
game itself, in the headless emulator, frame by frame at a fixed sixtieth of a second, with the top screen's
interface hidden. What changed on the way:
- **The pictures are the 3DS's own 400×240**, scaled four times with sharp pixels. Azahar's high-resolution
  video dump needs an FFmpeg library its Linux build doesn't bundle (libavfilter 10), which would mean a
  download; so the game now records its own frames instead (the plan's fallback, and an honest look: real
  old-3DS pixels). If you'd like the 4x emulator look later, say so: the same shot scripts can recapture it
  once that library is allowed in.
- **Your notes are in:** a "Runs on the original 2011 3DS" line on the end card; the Universal Updater line
  held back until it's listed.
- **The script and captions** (docs/plan/trailer-script.md): about 860 characters of narration, warm and
  short, the captions carrying the beats.
