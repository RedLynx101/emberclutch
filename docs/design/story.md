# Skyreach Valley: the story bible

*Proposal, 2026-09-30, from Noah's two pages of quest notes. Nothing here is built yet. Once Noah
approves it (the storyboard page takes his Keep / Change marks), the approved parts go into
`docs/plan/decisions.md` and the pass in `docs/plan/living-valley.md` builds them.*

Quest names marked **(kept)** stay as they are, **(changed)** keep their place but start, play or end
differently, and **(new)** are new. "Q1" to "Q8" are today's quests (see core/campaign.cpp).

## 1. The rules every quest follows (from Noah's notes)

1. **No quiet quests.** Every quest starts with someone asking, in person or by letter, or with
   something you can see happen. Nothing starts silently in the Journal.
2. **Every quest ends with a person.** You go back to whoever asked. They react, and they tell you a
   little more about themselves or the valley (the "return" talk).
3. **Everything makes sense.** Each step says who wants it and why. A step never asks for something
   the game hasn't shown you how to do.
4. **Every line has a feeling.** Each line of dialogue carries an emotion that sets the speaker's
   eyes, mouth, body and voice, and can pop an emote over their head with a sound (section 8).
5. **The mailbox carries the world.** Invitations, follow-ups, thank-yous, gifts and news of new
   things in later versions all come as letters to the den's mailbox (section 7).
6. **Keep it cute and fun.** Goofy where it can be, warm underneath.

## 2. The world (lore that fills today's gaps)

- **Skyreach Valley** is a green valley ringed by peaks, with floating isles over Mirror Lake and the
  falls beside your den. People here raise dragons from eggs and live alongside them. It's the first
  valley of many: others lie beyond the gap, past the Wanderers' Trailhead (the later regions).
- **Why the lanterns are dark.** Each festival lantern has a heart of **emberglass**, which holds a
  dragon's flame for one year. For forty years Old Rowan's dragon **Cinder** relit every one of them
  before the festival. This year Cinder is too old: he sleeps by the Lodge's hearth and snores smoke
  rings. So the valley needs a new keeper and a young dragon. That's you.
- **The star dragon.** Long ago a dragon fell out of the night sky onto the hill where the Nesting
  Stone stands, lost and too tired to lift its wings. The valley lit every lantern it had, and on
  the last night the dragon rose and followed the lights home. The Lantern Festival has lit the way
  every year since, and some say it still comes back to look.
- **Star shards** fall in a meadow past the gap. They're warm like a heartbeat, and a shard held to
  emberglass lights it. Sable thinks they're scales the star dragon sheds when it passes.
- **The Nesting Stone** is where the star dragon landed. It stays warm at night. Pairs settle there,
  and eggs laid near it are said to be lucky.
- **The Cold Vault** was dug by the first keepers to keep eggs asleep through hard winters.
- **Frostspire Hollow.** Wild dragons gather in the ice caves to grow strong before they cross the
  peaks. They test visitors for sport, not anger. Tove keeps count, as her teacher did before her.
- **Moonpetal Glade.** Moonpetals open only for dragons that shine. Celestine's grandmother started
  the pageant to celebrate dragons' beauty, and Celestine has run it for twelve years with
  ferocious devotion.
- **Emberpeak Caldera** is the league's grand stage, on a volcano's rim.

### Chapter 1: A letter by the door

**Rowan's invitation** (a letter, new). Arrives when your first egg hatches:
> Dear new keeper, word travels fast in a small valley, and word is there's a brand-new dragon at the
> den by the falls! Come up to the Lodge and say hello; I'll put the kettle on. Bring your little
> one. It's dangerous to go alone, you know. - Old Rowan. P.S. Mind the third step. It wobbles.

If you've already found Rowan, the letter never comes.

## 8. Feelings: how a line looks and sounds

Each line of dialogue starts with a feeling tag, `[happy]` for example (untagged lines are calm). The
tag sets:

| Tag | Eyes | Mouth | Body | Emote | Sound | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| calm | open, blinking | smile | idle, talk | | | as is |
| happy | smiling arcs | open smile | little bounce | notes | a chime | a touch higher |
| laugh | shut arcs | wide open | laugh (shoulders shake) | notes | giggle blips | quick, bouncing |
| excited | sparkles | wide open | bounce, fists up | sparkles | a trill | higher, faster |
| surprised | wide, small pupils | "o" | step back | ! | a pop | a jump up |
| shock | wider, shaking | big "o" | jump, hands up | !! | a sting | a squeak, then fast |
| sad | droopy lids | small frown | shoulders drop | gloom cloud | a droop | lower, slower |
| crying | shut, tears | wobble | sniffle, rub eyes | tears | sniffles | wobbly |
| angry | slanted lids | frown | stomp | anger mark | a puff | lower, louder, faster |
| huff | half-lidded, looking away | pout | arms crossed | a puff of steam | "hmph" | clipped |
| worried | raised inner brows | wobbly | hands together | sweat drop | a gulp | quieter |
| scared | wide, trembling | small "o" | shiver | sweat drops | a shiver | shaky |
| sleepy | half shut | yawn | sway, yawn | zzz | a yawn whistle | slow |
| love | hearts | smile | hands to cheeks, sway | hearts | a sparkle | warm, higher |
| proud | shut, head up | grin | hands on hips | sparkles | a fanfare blip | bold |
| cool | half-lidded | smirk | lean back, arms crossed | | a low "heh" | low, slow |
| shy | looking down | small smile | sway, hands behind | ... | a little blip | quiet |
| thinking | looking up | flat | hand to chin | ? | a "hm?" | slower |
| wistful | soft, looking away | soft smile | look to the sky | | a soft chime | slow, warm |
| dizzy | spirals | wobble | wobble | stars circling | a wobble | wobbly |

The emote sounds are synthesized (short, soft blips and chimes, in the Animal Crossing spirit). People
also show feelings without talking: they turn and wave as you pass; Celestine gasps at a muddy dragon;
each has an idle habit (Rowan sips tea, Maple counts coins, Bram pets Custard, Fig dozes, Tam casts,
Tove counts on her fingers, Wren checks her clipboard, Celestine strikes poses, Rook broods).
