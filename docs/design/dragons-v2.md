# Dragons, Version 2: Thirty-Six Kinds (D76–D77)

Status: **decided in outline, 2026-09-25** (Noah's R11 answers, D77); the open points at the
end go to Noah before the work starts. It replaces the dragons' side of
[breeds & genetics](breeds-and-genetics.md) and the looks of D54 as the new dragons land;
until then the old dragons stay in the game.

## 1. The idea
Every kind of dragon is **genuinely unique**: its own silhouette, body, baby, egg, way of
moving and flying, in the storybook look of a cozy life-sim ([look and feel](look-and-feel.md))
crossed with a dragon film's lovable, readable dragons, and grown adults that are cool and
majestic. Time and care go into every kind; nothing is a recolour of another.

## 2. The roster: 8 base breeds, 28 crossbreeds
- **Base breeds:** eight, each its own design. The first four are R11's: the **Pouncer**
  (sleek, cat-like), the **Puffback** (round, gentle giant), the **Crestwing** (feathered,
  elegant) and the **Ribbontail** (long, serpentine). Four more are designed later (R13).
  Names follow each design (Noah: "whatever makes sense for the breed design"); these four
  keep their working names unless a better one comes up while they're built.
- **Crossbreeds:** every pair of different base breeds has its own crossbreed, designed and
  modelled as a kind of its own (not a blend of parts): 28 when all eight exist.
- **36 kinds** in all (8 + 28). The old dragons (six elements, 21 breeds, the looks) are
  replaced (D77).

## 3. Variants: four of each kind
- **Three common variants:** the same model, different painted textures (colourings).
- **One rare variant:** a rarer, more special version with a slightly different model (a
  bigger crest, extra fins, glowing marks) and its own texture. It's the successor of the
  wild look.
- 36 kinds × 4 variants = **144** Dragondex entries.

## 4. Genetics
- A dragon carries **two breed alleles**, each one of the eight bases. The same allele twice
  is that base breed; two different ones are their crossbreed. A crossbreed passes one of
  its two alleles to each egg, so two crossbreeds can give either base or a new crossbreed,
  as the elements do now.
- The variant is inherited like the looks are (usually a parent's, sometimes a fresh roll),
  and the rare variant stays rare.
- Sex, size (a little each way within the breed's size) and the heartglow carry over.
- **Sizes between breeds:** up to ±50% from the smallest grown breed to the largest (Noah:
  "+-50% from smallest to largest"); the den, beds and cameras frame each by its size.

## 5. Stats, manners and traits (in the way of Pokémon or Palworld)
- **Base stats per breed:** each breed has its own base values, and each dragon its own
  potential on top (fixed at hatching), grown by care and training. **Rarer dragons have
  better base stats** (crossbreeds above bases, the rare variant above its kind).
- **Manners:** the personality every dragon already has (Brave, Shy, Playful, Proud, Sleepy,
  Curious), each breed **prone to some** more than others.
- **Traits:** passive abilities (for example *Swift* flies faster, *Treasure Nose* finds
  more on the Wanderings, *Night Owl* is livelier after dark, *Sturdy* tires slowly, *Tidy*
  stays clean), from common to very rare. **Each breed is prone to some traits**, and the
  rarer dragons get at **rarer traits**. Traits are inherited with a chance from the parents.
- Open: the stat list, how many traits, more manners (below).

## 6. Bodies and animation
- **Different skeletons are welcome** (Noah), as long as every body has **the same kinds
  of animation**: the same list of clips (idle, walk, run, sit, sleep, eat, the games, the
  care, the flight set…) each made for that body. A Ribbontail's sit is a coil; a
  Puffback's run is a trundle; a Crestwing's pounce is a hop and a flourish.
- **Body plans:** each base breed gets its own skeleton and its own full set of clips, grown
  and baby. A crossbreed uses the body plan that suits its design (usually a parent's, with
  its own proportions and parts), or a new plan if its design needs one, with the full set.
- **Every dragon can be ridden** (D77), each flying its own way: the Pouncer quick and
  agile, the Puffback slow and steady on small fast wings, the Crestwing gliding far, the
  Ribbontail weaving. Every body plan has a rider's seat and the flight clips.
- **Babies:** each kind has its own baby body, changing into the young body at the first
  molt as now (D36).
- **Eggs:** each kind has its own egg (shape and pattern), in its colours, so an egg hints
  at what hatches.
- **Eyes:** big and glossy, with pupils that change with mood: round and soft when happy,
  narrow when startled or cross.

## 7. Art and budgets
- The new look: hand-painted soft textures (gradients, stripes, spots, moss, feathers), no
  fine scale detail; the heartglow as now.
- Built by script in Blender, as now: a shared library (rig helpers, the growth stages, the
  exporter, the render sheets, the checks) and one script per kind.
- Budgets as the den has them: a grown dragon about 3,000 triangles at full detail (a lower
  detail level for the rest), babies less; 51 clips today (more come with Beta's flying).
  Models and clips load by kind as needed, so only the dragons you keep cost memory.

## 8. Your dragons today
Each dragon in a save (and each egg) becomes **a random kind in a common variant**, fixed
by who it is (the same result every time), keeping its name, bond, age, stage, needs,
personality and history (D77, Noah's 3B). While only the first kinds exist, the pick is
among those.
